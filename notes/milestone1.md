# Milestone 1 — Community and candidate distinctions

## Community

**r/nba** — https://www.reddit.com/r/nba

- Public, no invite or login needed to read.
- Not built around one show or release; it covers the whole league.
- High volume, with real variation in quality, from one-line reactions to
  paragraph-long arguments.
- I'm reading **comments**, not submissions. Most r/nba submissions are a
  headline plus a link; the takes live in the comments.

## What I read

Comments from 2026-10-20, opening night of the season: the live 76ers–Knicks
and Thunder–Spurs game threads, and the thread on The Athletic's preseason
power rankings.

## Candidate distinctions

### 1. Some comments cite a checkable number or fact. Most don't.

> "We had the #3 defense last year and the #8 offense along with a top-5 MVP
> Candidate... we addressed this summer by bringing in 3 40%+ shooters. This
> team which just had a 60-win season is now younger, better fitting..."

> "Why the hell are the Wolves 12th on this list?? That's INSANE"

Both are about the same rankings. One gives you something to check; the other
gives you volume.

### 2. Some give a reason for their verdict. Others just state the verdict.

A reason doesn't have to be a number. It can be a conditional or a mechanism.

> "We're basically 100% dependent on health. If AG and Braun stay healthy then
> our defense doesn't suffer and we're a top team. If not then we're probably
> in the 5-7 range in the West"

> "There are only two spots the Spurs should be in on this list, either 1 or 2."

This is close to #1 but not the same: the health comment has no stat, yet it
explains *why*.

### 3. Some react to what's happening right now. Others are about the season.

> "Gotta love the Rihanna blasting, down by 6 with under a minute to go at
> home."

> "Putting the Lakers in Tier 2 after an almost complete roster rebuild that
> wasn't even scheduled to be finished until next summer is a bold choice."

The game-thread comments only make sense if you're watching. The rankings
comments stand on their own.
